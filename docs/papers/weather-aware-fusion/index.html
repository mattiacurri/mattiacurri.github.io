Weather-Aware Multimodal Sensor Fusion for Robust Perception

M. Borgo, M. Curri, O. Zaroual, P. Serio, G. Pisaneschi, M. G. C. A. Cimino

2nd IEEE International Conference on Application of Information Technologies in Engineering, Management and Science

Summary

The paper fuses camera and radar data to detect vehicles in adverse weather, and evaluates the approach on the RADIATE dataset.

The research was carried out within the UIS Research Centre's Students for Italy's Future project. The PDF will be linked here once the proceedings are published.
